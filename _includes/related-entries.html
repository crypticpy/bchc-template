{%- comment -%} "Related" — the other entries that share the most facet values with this one. Scoring is deliberately simple and schema-driven: for every other entry, count the values it shares with this entry across all `facet: true` fields. The highest scores win; when fewer than `limit` entries share anything, the most recently published entries top the list up. Bounded to one pass over the pool per field — fine for a few hundred entries, which is the size this template targets.
