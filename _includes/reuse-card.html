{%- comment -%} One sidebar card for a schema group — "Reuse this", "Contact", or any other group the entry page hands it. Renders every field of that group that has a value and has not already been shown elsewhere on the page, as a label/value list. Emits nothing when nothing is left, so the rail never shows a labelled void.
