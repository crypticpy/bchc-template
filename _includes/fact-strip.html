{%- comment -%} "Is this reusable for us?" — the at-a-glance strip at the top of an entry page. Renders every field whose effective card slot is `icon` (the signal fields), plus any `card: meta` field that has not already been shown in the page header. Values carry their `option_meta` icon, `description` (as a tooltip) and `warn` tone. Facts with no value are skipped; the whole strip disappears when nothing is left. The cells share one grid row, so the tallest fact sets every cell's height. Each cell therefore shows at most `cap` values and trails a "+n" counter — the chip idiom from entry-card.html — naming the hidden values in its tooltip and to a screen reader. The cap is 3, not the cards' 2: this is the page's authoritative signal list, and at rail-narrowed column widths a trailing "+n" wraps onto a line of its own, so a lower cap would hide a fact without buying back a row. Row alignment is handled in CSS (.fact subgrids the label and value rows).
